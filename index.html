Presentation at the 2026 International Meeting of the Psychometric Society

I recently presented our research, Pairwise Target Rotation for Factor Models, in an oral session at the 2026 International Meeting of the Psychometric Society (IMPS) held at Yonsei University in Seoul, South Korea back in July 2026. This work focuses on developing an intuitive yet flexible method for incorporating a priori information, such as item semantics, into factor rotations. The approach can be used to produce interpretable factor solutions and effectively characterize latent variables.

Poster at the 2026 American Educational Research Association Annual Meeting

Our work, Design and Implementation of Reduced Longitudinal Experiments, was selected from over 13,000 submissions for poster presentation at the 2026 American Educational Research Association (AERA) Annual Meeting. It focuses on characterizing hierarchical linear models to produce practical recommendations for designing and implementing longitudinal experiments. My co-author, Le Dolino, presented our paper at the conference back in April 2026 in Los Angeles, California.

// 02. Selected Works

Research & Projects

Click on the icon(s) at the top right of each card for more details.

Pairwise Target Rotation for Factor Models

We developed a new factor rotation method (an unsupervised learning technique) and its Python implementation, that can incorporate arbitrary prior information (e.g., semantics) for exploration and model building.

Presented at the 2026 International Meeting of the Psychometric Society (IMPS) in an oral session.

Won 1st Place Best Graduate Student Paper Award at 25th Student-Faculty Conference on Statistical Sciences.

Impact: New method (unsupervised learning) that can be used to effectively uncover latent structures.

Design and Implementation of Reduced Longitudinal Experiments

We characterized hierarchical linear models through the design and analysis of a Monte Carlo experiment (including the simulation of nested data, attrition, and more), implemented in R, to produce recommendations for longitudinal experiments.

Presented at the 2026 American Educational Research Association (AERA) Annual Meeting in a poster session.

Impact: Useful practical recommendations for designing and implementing robust longitdunal experiments in resource-limited contexts.

FactorFlow: A Visual Analytics Workspace with Large Language Model-Assisted Interpretation for Factor Analysis

I developed an LLM-enhanced visual workbench for exploratory factor analysis with interactive model building and automated interpretation.

Impact: End-to-end workspace for exploratory factor analysis with comprehensive visualizations and automated interpretation.

Match App

I implemented Gower’s Distance and Binary Integer Linear Programming using R and created a web app for a student council initiative that involved optimally grouping students for theses.

Impact: Optimal and automated groupings, ensuring compatible work styles and research interests among students.

Simulated Normality Test

For a course project in an undergraduate class, we explored an alternative test for normality. I also made an R package implementing the test. We leveraged Monte Carlo simulations to characterize the null distribution of the test statistic after establishing its ancillarity.

Impact: Exploration of alternative test for normality.

TREE$

I made a game for one of my courses during my first semester in college. The game was made using Unity, with C# as the programming language. The simple design assets were made using Microsoft PowerPoint while the background music was created using Chrome Music Lab - Song Maker.

Impact: Delivered core message of position paper creatively.
